Theory

A turbine (or Hall-effect) flow sensor has a rotor turned by the liquid. Each blade passing the sensor gives one pulse.
Pulse frequency f = K × Q (here K = 7.5 Hz per L/min)
Flow Q = f / K, volume V = N / 450 litres
At very low flow, friction makes the sensor under-read, so the error is largest there.
Procedure
  1. Turn the pump on and set a valve opening.
  2. Press Start. For 30 s of test time the tank collects water and the pulses are counted.
  3. Press Add to table to compare the reference flow (V/t) with the sensor flow (f / 7.5).
  4. Repeat at valve openings from 10% to 100%.
  5. Read the graph: the points follow the dashed ideal line, and the gap at low flow shows the sensor error.
