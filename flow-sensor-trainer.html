Working principle

1. Pressure generationThe foot pump raises pressure in the tank; the Bourdon gauge shows it directly.
2. ConversionThe strain-gauge transducer and signal conditioner give a voltage proportional to pressure.
3. IndicationThe digital indicator converts the conditioned voltage back to a pressure reading.

Ideal relationship: 0 psi → 0 V, 100 psi → 5 V, so sensitivity = 5 / 100 = 0.05 V/psi.

Safety and accuracy: This is a generic educational simulation, not a reproduction of any manufacturer’s circuit. Real calibration, wiring and display scaling may differ. Never exceed the pressure rating of the physical trainer and follow your laboratory manual.
