3. Theory

Resistances below about 1 Ω are measured with a four-terminal standard and unknown, joined by a short thick link (yoke) of resistance r. The second pair of ratio arms, p and q, removes the effect of this link.

Rx = S·(P/Q) + [ q·r / (p + q + r) ] · (P/Q − p/q)

When the two ratios are made equal, P/Q = p/q, the second term is zero and the result does not depend on r:

Rx = S · P/Q

4. Procedure

  1. Select the ratio P/Q = p/q and the yoke resistance r.
  2. Adjust the standard resistor S with the slider and the step buttons until the galvanometer reads zero.
  3. Press Record reading. Rx = S × P/Q is entered in the table and compared with the actual value.
  4. Increase the inner arm mismatch and null the bridge again. Note how the yoke resistance now changes the result.
  5. Press New unknown Rx and repeat. If no balance is possible, change the ratio.
