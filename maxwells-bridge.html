3. Theory

The unknown coil (inductance Lx, resistance Rx) forms one arm. The opposite arm is a resistor R4 in parallel with a standard capacitor C4. Balance of the bridge requires Z1Z4 = Z2Z3, which gives two real equations:

Lx = R2 R3 C4
Rx = R2 R3 / R4
Q = ωLx / Rx = ωC4R4

C4 sets the inductive balance and R4 sets the resistive balance. The two settings interact only weakly, so the null is found by adjusting them in turn. The bridge is supplied at 1 kHz.

4. Procedure

  1. Choose R2 and R3. A larger R2R3 brings the capacitor C4 into a practical range.
  2. Adjust C4 and R4 in turn, with the sliders and the step buttons, until the detector output is a minimum and the status reads Balanced.
  3. Press Record reading. Lx and Rx are calculated from the balance equations and compared with the actual values.
  4. Press New unknown coil and repeat with other values of R2 and R3.
