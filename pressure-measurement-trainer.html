Working principle

1. Pressure generation
The foot pump increases pressure in the tank. The analog gauge shows the simulated tank pressure.
2. Conversion
The strain-gauge transducer and signal conditioner produce a voltage proportional to pressure.
3. Indication
The digital indicator converts the conditioned voltage into a pressure reading.

Ideal relationship: 0 psi → 0 V; 100 psi → 5 V; sensitivity = 5/100 = 0.05 V/psi.

Safety and accuracy: This is a generic educational virtual kit, not a verified reproduction of any manufacturer's circuit. Actual equipment calibration, wiring and display scaling may differ. Never exceed the real trainer's pressure rating, and follow your laboratory manual when operating physical equipment.
