Two-Wattmeter Method: Power Measurement in a 3-Phase Circuit

A balanced three-phase load is connected to a three-phase supply. Change the load current and power factor and watch W1 and W2.

W1 (W)0
W2 (W)0
W1 + W2 (W)0

Theory

For a balanced load with lagging angle φ: W1 = VL IL cos(30° − φ), W2 = VL IL cos(30° + φ)
Total power P = W1 + W2 = √3 VL IL cos φ
Power factor tan φ = √3 (W1 − W2) / (W1 + W2), and cos φ = cos(tan⁻¹ of that)
At pf = 0.5 (φ = 60°) W2 = 0. Beyond that W2 reads negative, so its pressure coil connections are reversed and its reading is taken as negative. For a leading load, W1 and W2 swap roles.
